CineVerse – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

App Name: CineVerse

Developer: Afaq Saleem

CineVerse ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when you register via Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage Data: App settings, watchlist, favorites, and reviews stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Device Token: Firebase Cloud Messaging token for push notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize app features (watchlist, favorites, reviews).
  • To send push notifications about new movie/TV show releases (only if you opt in).
  • To sync your settings across devices.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database): Movie and TV show data. See TMDB Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage, and notifications. See Firebase Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Firebase servers. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

5. Notifications

Push notifications are optional. You can enable or disable them at any time from the app's Settings screen.

6. Children's Privacy

CineVerse is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

CineVerse – 隐私政策

CineVerse（"我们"、"我们的"或"我们"）致力于保护您的隐私。本隐私政策解释了当您使用我们的移动应用程序时，我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 账户信息： 通过Firebase Authentication注册时的电子邮件地址和显示名称。
  • 使用数据： 存储在Firebase Firestore中的应用程序设置、观看列表、收藏夹和评论。
  • 设备令牌： 用于推送通知的Firebase Cloud Messaging令牌。

2. 我们如何使用您的信息

  • 提供和个性化应用程序功能（观看列表、收藏夹、评论）。
  • 发送有关新电影/电视节目发布的推送通知（仅在您选择加入时）。
  • 在设备之间同步您的设置。

3. 第三方服务

我们使用以下第三方服务：

  • TMDB（The Movie Database）： 电影和电视节目数据。请参阅TMDB隐私政策。

4. 数据存储

您的数据安全地存储在Firebase服务器上。我们不会向第三方出售或共享您的个人数据。

5. 通知

推送通知是可选的。您可以随时从应用程序的设置屏幕启用或禁用它们。
